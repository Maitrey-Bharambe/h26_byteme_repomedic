# Code of Conduct

This project follows the spirit of the
[Contributor Covenant, version 2.1](https://www.contributor-covenant.org/version/2/1/code_of_conduct/).

## Our standards

- Be respectful and welcoming to people of every background and experience level.
- Give and accept constructive feedback gracefully; critique code, not people.
- Assume good intent, and ask before assuming.
- No harassment, discrimination, personal attacks, trolling or publishing others' private
  information.

## Scope

These rules apply in all project spaces (issues, pull requests, discussions and reviews) and
when you represent the project elsewhere.

## Enforcement

Report unacceptable behaviour to the maintainers privately. Use the contact listed on the
repository, or GitHub's private reporting if no contact is listed. All reports are reviewed
and kept confidential. Maintainers may remove, edit or reject contributions, and may
temporarily or permanently ban contributors who violate this code.
